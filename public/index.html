OUR BASELINE

Safety & policies

Safety features are a foundation, not a substitute for legal review and ongoing operations.

01

Adults only

Accounts require a date of birth, but public launch requires a real age-assurance provider. The developer bypass is blocked in production.

02

Consent & rights

Every upload requires attestations for adult performers, informed consent, and the right to distribute. Keep signed releases and identity/consent records securely outside public storage.

03

Moderation first

New uploads stay private until a moderator approves them. Moderation decisions and report actions are audit-logged.

04

Report & remove

Report suspected non-consensual content, underage-safety concerns, copyright issues, privacy violations, or other abuse. Treat urgent reports as time-sensitive.

!
Not ready for unrestricted public launch

Configure external age assurance, email delivery, secure object storage/CDN, trained moderators, emergency escalation and takedown procedures, privacy/retention policies, and jurisdiction-specific legal review first.
